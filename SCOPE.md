# Final-Year Project Scope

## Title
**INTEGRATED CHURCH MANAGEMENT AND AI-ASSISTED ADMINISTRATION SYSTEM**

## In Scope
- Member Management
- Guest Management
- Attendance Management
- Church Giving Management
- Asset Management
- AI-Assisted Administration
- SMS and Notifications
- Reports and Dashboard
- External/Bezaleli Integration Layer
- User Management and Security

## Explicitly Out of Scope
- Department Management
- Mobile application
- Online payment gateway
- Biometric attendance
- Facial recognition
- Multi-church management

The `legacy/out_of_scope/` directory contains inactive legacy code retained only for historical/reference purposes. It is not routed or part of the active application.
